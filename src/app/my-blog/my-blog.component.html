Page title hierarchy

According to WCAG guidelines, it is essential to organize page headings in a logical and coherent manner to clearly structure the content. A page should start with an H1 representing the main title, followed by H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections, and so on, without skipping levels, to ensure a clear and understandable hierarchy. Each section should begin with an appropriate heading, which facilitates navigation, especially for users of screen readers, and allows the content’s organization to be quickly grasped. Maintaining this hierarchy consistently across the site improves accessibility and orientation. To ensure that the structure of my headings follows these best practices, I used the HeadingsMaps plugin, which allows you to instantly visualize the hierarchy of a page’s headings and verify its compliance.
